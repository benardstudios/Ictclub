About ICT Club Uganda

Inspiring innovation, creativity, leadership and technological excellence since 2026.

Who We Are

A Community of Young Innovators

ICT Club Uganda is a student-led technology community dedicated to helping learners acquire practical digital skills, programming knowledge, robotics experience and innovative thinking.

Through workshops, projects, competitions and mentorship, members gain real-world experience that prepares them for Uganda's growing digital economy and the global technology landscape.

Our History

The Club Journey

From a small group to a thriving technology community.

2023

Foundation

ICT Club officially launched with a founding group of enthusiastic students and a passionate patron teacher.

2024

First Programmes

Introduced structured programming and robotics training sessions. First student websites developed.

2025

Competition Debut

Participated in district ICT competitions and exhibitions. Launched cybersecurity and networking tracks.

2026

Expansion & Recognition

Expanded to 250+ members. Launched this digital platform, won district innovation award, and established financial literacy integration.
