Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join ICT Club Uganda?

Register through the Student Portal. You'll need a school email address. Membership is free for all enrolled students.

Do I need prior experience?

No! We welcome complete beginners. Our lessons are designed to take you from zero knowledge to building real projects.

Are lessons free for members?

Yes, all lessons, workshops and resources on this platform are free for registered ICT Club Uganda members.
